# Vendor data clause (DRAFT text for counsel)

For the signed vendor agreement (`vendor-document-requirements.md` item 8). Plain drafting to start
the conversation; counsel owns the final text and the country-specific variants.

## 1. Roles
For a guest's booking, the vendor and Caribbean VIP each decide how that guest's data is used for
their own purposes, so each is a controller of its own use. Caribbean VIP is not the vendor's
processor.

## 2. What the vendor receives
Guest name, party size, booked slot, and any note the guest chose to add. Nothing else. The vendor
does not receive the guest's email, location or payment details.

## 3. What the vendor may do with it
Use it only to deliver the booked excursion and to meet the vendor's own legal duties. No marketing
to the guest, no sale, no sharing with others except as needed to deliver the excursion.

## 4. Security and staff
Keep the data safe, limit it to staff who need it, and remove a leaver's access the same day.

## 5. Scanning and logging
Every voucher scan is logged with the scanner's account and time, and an admin may review unusual
patterns. Vendor staff must be told this.

## 6. Retention and erasure
Delete guest data when the excursion is delivered and any dispute window has closed
**[COUNSEL: period]**. When Caribbean VIP tells the vendor a guest asked for erasure, the vendor
deletes what it holds about that guest unless the law requires it to keep it.

## 7. Breaches
Tell Caribbean VIP without delay, and within **[COUNSEL: hours]**, of any loss or leak of guest data.
